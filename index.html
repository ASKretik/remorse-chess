Remorse Chess

Chess, but every capture haunts the capturer.

  • All normal chess rules apply.
  • A piece that captures feels remorse and can't capture again — and can't give check.
  • Remorse is lifted when a bishop moves to a square orthogonally adjacent (up, down, left, right — not diagonal) to the remorseful piece.
  • Capture again, and the remorse returns.

Rules

A capturing piece becomes remorseful (red ring): no captures, no check. Any bishop (either colour) landing orthogonally next to it lifts the remorse. Everything else is standard chess.
